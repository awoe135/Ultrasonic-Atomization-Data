# Experimental Data for Ultrasonic Atomization

This repository contains experimental measurement datasets associated with our research on non-contact ultrasonic atomization.

## Data files

**1. Impedance analyzer data.xls**

Electrical impedance measurements of the ultrasonic transducer, used to characterize its electrical response and resonant behavior.

**2. Laser vibrometer data.csv**

Laser vibrometer measurements of the ultrasonic transducer, used to characterize its vibration response.

## Data availability

These datasets are provided to support the experimental characterization results reported in the associated research article.
